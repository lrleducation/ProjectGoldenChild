# DPIA starter

Project Golden Child intends to process children's identity/contact information and, for direct parent/carer registrations, special-category health information about childhood cancer. Before opening the live registration form, document at minimum: purpose and necessity, lawful basis and Article 9 condition, consent/withdrawal process where consent is relied upon, data minimisation, access roles, retention/deletion, data-subject rights, processor locations/contracts, breach response, safeguarding implications, risks to children and bereaved families, and mitigations.

The third-party referral route deliberately collects minimal information and should not be used to collect detailed medical information. The family should make the decision about whether to provide health data themselves.
