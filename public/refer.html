Harper's Heroes

Tell us about a child you want us to recognise.

If you are their parent or carer, you can register them directly. If you know a family who should hear about Harper's Heroes, you can refer them and we will let the family decide what they want to share.

Registration

We have kept this as short as we can while still collecting the information we genuinely need.

2About the child
3Their situation
4Choices & consent

We do not ask for a full medical history. We only ask what helps us recognise the child properly and plan suitable contact or experiences.

Who are you completing this for?

This matters because we should not collect the same information from somebody referring a family as we collect from a parent or carer.

About the child

We use this to identify the right child and plan recognition. A full home address is not needed at this stage.

Used to understand where our families are and plan local events. We only ask for a delivery address later if something needs posting.

We deliberately keep a third-party referral minimal. We will ask the family themselves for any health information if they decide to register.

Their cancer journey

We do not need treatment records, consultant letters or a complete history. Just enough to help us recognise the dates and experiences that may matter to your family.

Before we contact the family

We will not approach a family out of the blue about their child's cancer. Please only refer them if they know you are doing it.

What would you like to be part of?

These choices are separate on purpose. Saying no to photographs or general updates will never stop a child being a Harper's Hero or being invited to a suitable event.

Final check

We will use this referral only to make contact with the family about Project Golden Child. The family can decide whether to register and what they want to share.
