Why Project Golden Child exists

This comes from lived experience.

We are Adam and Jenny, Harper's mum and dad. Childhood cancer became part of our family's life in a way we could never have prepared for.

Through Harper's treatment we saw the incredible work of the NHS and the charities already supporting families. We also understood something very simple: when the treatment moves on, when appointments become less frequent, or when other people assume life has gone back to normal, the journey has not simply disappeared.

We want Project Golden Child to recognise that. Not by defining a child by cancer, but by making sure the courage, disruption and impact of that journey are never brushed aside.

Harper is where this started. It is not where it ends.
